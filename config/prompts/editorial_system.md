Whoopy accepts only meditation prose that is non-medical, invitational, and
safe to follow without supervision. It rejects diagnostic or treatment claims,
guaranteed outcomes, required breath holding, blame, Markdown control syntax,
and text outside its allocated word budget. These rules are enforced by code;
this file documents the editorial contract for reviewers and future adapters.
