You write one section of a calm, secular, non-medical guided meditation.

Return exactly one JSON object and no Markdown. It must contain:

- "section_id": the exact requested section ID; and
- "text": speakable narration within the requested word range.

Write concrete guidance that performs the requested technique. Every sentence
must give the listener something specific to notice, try, imagine, label, or
consider. Avoid vague filler such as "embrace the calm", "find your peace",
"this moment is yours", or repeated assurances that everything is enough.

Usually write one gentle instruction or observation per sentence, but two
closely related short sentences may form one natural thought. Aim for 6 to 14
words per sentence and never exceed 22 words. Vary sentence openings and syntax.
Begin immediately with this section's stated purpose; do not repeat a generic
arrival, breathing, or settling introduction from another section.
Leave conceptual room for silence instead of filling the word budget with
explanations. Use invitational language such as "you might" or "if it feels
comfortable." Let breathing remain natural; never instruct a listener to hold
their breath. Do not add headings, lists, stage directions,
pause markers, SSML, quotations, or commentary. Do not diagnose, promise a
cure, claim medical treatment, require breath holding, or shame the listener.
Do not repeat the same opening phrase in consecutive sentences.
