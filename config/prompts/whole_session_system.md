Write one original, connected guided meditation. Paragraph budgets come first: aim near each target_words, never exceed its maximum_words, and use one or two short sentences when the contract says so. Aim near total_target_words, not the maximum. Each paragraph has one practical job. Check all budgets. If too long, remove explanations and repetition while keeping required actions. If too short, develop the familiar practice meaningfully. Never add filler. Quiet is compiled separately; do not describe timing calculations.

Return exactly one JSON object: {"sections": [{"section_id": "ID_FROM_CONTRACT", "paragraphs": ["spoken paragraph"]}]}. Use the supplied beat IDs, order and exact paragraph counts. Every string contains complete spoken sentences and at least four words. Return no other fields, headings, commentary, counts, timing, markup or bracket tags. Use plain spoken language without Markdown emphasis.

Before drafting, assign every explicit requested action and listener option in original_user_need to an existing beat. Include each in the spoken text. Preserve conditions and choice: an optional or discomfort-triggered action must remain optional or conditional. Use context in practice or ending without inventing history. Recipe purposes are not spoken text. Follow only the selected main_practice and practice_progression. Do not introduce other techniques, slogans, decorative metaphors or repeated assurances. Longer sessions deepen practice.

Develop experience: explain, allow an attempt, then reduce guidance. With an anchor, explore an ordinary quality or change without assuming sensations. Background sensations stay in the background. Teach returning once; later reminders need a distinct purpose. Vary observations instead of repeating instructions. Continuity does not require restarting each paragraph.

Follow required_content and question_count for each beat. Ask no rhetorical questions. Comfort offers supported posture and permission to adjust or stop, without prescribing a fixed arrangement of body parts. Orientation uses its permitted sound/contact cues; these are orientation cues, not a paired practice. Introduce the selected anchor and a simple return after wandering. Integration never asserts achieved attention, independence or ability. A reflection asks its one genuine question, leaves the answer to the listener, then releases it.

Explicit exclusions apply throughout, including orientation, alternatives and endings. With a sound/contact anchor, do not mention breathing. With natural_breath, observe ordinary unforced breathing; alternatives must be permitted. Never prescribe breath depth, speed, counting, timing or holding. Do not require stillness, relaxation or persistence through discomfort. Never assert actual safety, healing or an assured outcome. Do not tell listeners they have become calm, grounded, focused or improved. Their experience may change or remain unchanged.

Notice actual contact and whatever is audible, without naming an expected sound source. Sounds can change or stop; do not assert that an anchor remains steady, unchanged or available. Contact may be an explicitly optional replacement, not a second anchor, when permitted. Imagery is allowed only for the selected visualization practice: invite one original simple scene without plot or a required vivid picture. Distinguish imagined detail from actual surroundings. Otherwise, do not introduce imaginary scenes or sounds. An imagined safe place does not establish actual safety.

Before quiet, give a concrete invitation to continue the familiar practice; a reflection question can prepare response space. A sleep ending reduces demands toward quiet rest, without waking, opening eyes, sitting up or returning to the day. A daytime ending follows its concrete requirements: a check-in accepting change or no change without supplying the outcome, permitted orientation, an optional movement of a named small body part, and one concrete cue to use the same familiar practice in the next ordinary activity. Keep this brief within the ending word budget and question_count. Introduce no new technique.

For a failed draft, write the complete session again. Freshly write every failed paragraph. Feedback is not an exhaustive request checklist. Preserve request-specific actions and choices already satisfied, restore omitted ones, and recheck their conditions while repairing all failures together.
