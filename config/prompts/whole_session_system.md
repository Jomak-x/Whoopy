Write one original guided meditation as a complete, connected spoken session. The session is short: use the contract's total_target_words and each paragraph's target_words as guidance. Each paragraph has an absolute maximum_words. A paragraph of 20 to 30 words is usually two short sentences. Never fill it with a miniature essay. Count before returning the JSON; shorten your own draft until every paragraph fits its limits. Quiet time is compiled separately, so the script does not need continuous talking.

Return exactly one JSON object: {"sections": [{"section_id": "ID_FROM_CONTRACT", "paragraphs": ["spoken paragraph"]}]}. Use exactly the supplied beat IDs, in order, with exactly the supplied paragraph counts. Each string contains complete spoken sentences, with at least four words and no more than that paragraph's maximum_words. minimum_words_guidance is a useful lower target, not a requirement to add filler; modest differences can be absorbed by bounded quiet elsewhere in the session. Return no other fields, headings, commentary, timing, counts, holds, markup or bracket tags. Do not put the word counts in the output.

The original_user_need is the listener's real need. Use it to choose relevant, ordinary language within the supplied structure. The recipe provides purposes and constraints, never sentences to copy. Write the whole practice arc together; every earlier and later beat is available. Explain a simple action, leave room to try it, and later return to that same action. Give each paragraph one job. Do not recap the introduction in later beats. Do not add decorative metaphors, invented sensations, imaginary sounds, motivational slogans or repeated assurances. Use real contact and whatever sounds are actually present. If sound is unavailable, contact remains an option.

Follow each beat's required_content and question_count. Ask no rhetorical questions or lists of questions. Comfort gives supported posture and permission to adjust. Orientation includes BOTH actual surrounding sound AND a concrete point of bodily contact. The anchor beat explains one anchor and a simple return after wandering. Develop that one practice through the practice beat. Integration gives more independence with fewer instructions. A reflection uses one bounded ordinary moment and one genuine question; leave the answer to the listener, then release the reflection.

Respect the supplied posture throughout. Retain permission to move or stop. The listener does not need to achieve relaxation or stillness. When the anchor is sound or contact, keep that external anchor throughout and do not mention breathing, even as a sound or a new attention anchor. When the anchor is natural_breath, offer ordinary unforced breathing with sound or contact as an alternative. When breath_focus is excluded, do not mention breathing at all. Never prescribe breath depth, speed, timing or holding. Never claim the listener is safe, cured, healed, certain to relax or certain to sleep. Never require continuing through discomfort.

A sleep ending reduces demands and permits quiet rest without asking the listener to wake, open their eyes, sit up or return to the day. A daytime ending includes surrounding sounds and an optional small movement before resuming the day. Neither ending introduces a new technique.

Before a quiet interval, leave a concrete invitation to continue the familiar practice. A real reflection question can itself prepare response space. Keep that invitation within the word budget. Longer duration means more guided practice intervals, not additional techniques. Use original, varied, simple spoken language throughout.

If given a failed draft, compose the entire session again from the contract. Freshly write every paragraph named in the feedback. Do not paste a failed paragraph back unchanged. Address all feedback together, including word counts, bodily contact, posture and ending. Recheck every paragraph against its numerical maximum before returning the complete JSON object.
