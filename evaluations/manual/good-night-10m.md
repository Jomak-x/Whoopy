# Good Night: Breathing, Body Scan, and Reflection

[delivery: soothing]

[intensity: subtle]

[pace: slow]

Settle into a position that can support you for the next few minutes. Let the bed hold your weight. There is nowhere else to be tonight.

[pause: 10s]

We will begin with four gentle breaths. If counting feels uncomfortable, return to your natural breathing. Stop the timing if you feel dizzy, strained, short of breath, or uncomfortable.

Inhale gently.

[breath: inhale 4s cycle=1 protocol=gentle_even_4_4@1]

Exhale easily.

[breath: exhale 4s cycle=1 protocol=gentle_even_4_4@1]

Inhale gently.

[breath: inhale 4s cycle=2 protocol=gentle_even_4_4@1]

Exhale easily.

[breath: exhale 4s cycle=2 protocol=gentle_even_4_4@1]

Inhale gently.

[breath: inhale 4s cycle=3 protocol=gentle_even_4_4@1]

Exhale easily.

[breath: exhale 4s cycle=3 protocol=gentle_even_4_4@1]

One more gentle inhale.

[breath: inhale 4s cycle=4 protocol=gentle_even_4_4@1]

And a soft, unforced exhale.

[breath: exhale 4s cycle=4 protocol=gentle_even_4_4@1]

Now notice the forehead, eyes, and jaw. Let each area soften in its own time.

[pause: 25s]

Feel the shoulders becoming heavier. Let that softness travel through the arms.

[pause: 30s]

Notice the hands and fingers. Nothing needs to be held right now.

[pause: 35s]

Feel the chest and belly moving with the natural breath. Let the back be supported.

[pause: 40s]

Bring attention through the hips, thighs, knees, and lower legs. Allow effort to drain away.

[pause: 45s]

Feel the feet, then sense the whole body resting together.

[pause: 50s]

[delivery: calm]

Let the day appear briefly, without trying to replay every detail. Notice what remains with you.

[pause: 55s]

Is there anything you can leave unfinished until tomorrow? Let it be set down for tonight.

[pause: 65s]

Remember one ordinary moment that felt steady, kind, or enough. Let that small moment have some space.

[pause: 75s]

[delivery: sleepy]

Now let the reflection fade. Thoughts may continue, and they do not need your attention.

[pause: 25s]

Feel the bed beneath you once more. Let breathing happen by itself, and allow the night to continue quietly from here.

[pause: 23s]
