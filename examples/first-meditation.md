# First Local Meditation

Welcome. Let your attention arrive here, without needing to change anything.

[pause: 2s]

Notice where your body meets the chair or the floor. Let that support do a
little more of the work.

[pause: 3s]

Take an easy breath at your own pace. As you breathe out, allow your shoulders
to soften.

[pause: 3s]

When you are ready, gently bring your attention back to the room.
