# Whoopy mascot prompt set

These prompts are the reusable source for the current mascot family. All variants use the five supplied cat photos as identity reference and deliberately avoid a generic kitten or round cartoon-cat silhouette.

## Shared identity

Whoopy is the adult female cat in the six user-supplied reference photos refreshed on October 28, 2026. Her coat is muted brown-grey with broad near-black classic-tabby swirls, a dark forehead M and cheek markings, soft olive-gold almond eyes, a small pink-brown nose, white muzzle and chin, a white chest bib, and white socks. Preserve her natural adult proportions and slight facial asymmetry. Use the photos for identity, while keeping the animated 2D illustration style: confident flat shapes, clear silhouette, expressive poses, restrained linework, and transparent background. Avoid orange tiger coloring, photographic fur, painterly realism, kitten proportions, 3D rendering, or a generic stock mascot. The final Studio v7 reclining, seated, and sleeping assets use this updated identity; the older variants below are historical prompts.

## Logo

Three-quarter head and upper-bib portrait of the shared cat identity. Calm, alert expression; narrow mature face and tall ears must remain recognizable at 64px. Centered icon composition with transparent negative space.

## Seated

The shared cat sitting attentively upright with a slim long torso, long legs, curled tail, consistent tabby markings, white bib, and white front socks. Quiet, friendly expression; avoid a round or chubby silhouette.

## Sleeping

The shared cat resting in an elongated crescent pose with her head on her white paws. Keep the slim waist, long curved body, mature face, and consistent markings. Peaceful rather than baby-like; avoid a spherical curled-up shape.

## Concerned

The shared cat in a low compact but slim crouch, with a slight head tilt, ears gently angled outward, and calm thoughtful eyes. She should read as attentive and reassuring, never panicked, tearful, exaggerated, or chubby.

Final revision note: keep the torso visibly long, the waist narrow, the front legs extended slightly forward, and the tail relaxed along the ground. Render against uniform `#00FF00` for chroma-key alpha extraction; never use a checkerboard background.
